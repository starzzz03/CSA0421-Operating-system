=== Task 6 stage 4: design calculations (peak week 1) ===
Peak: 10000 streams x 5 Mb/s = 6250 MB/s = 50 Gb/s
Library: 20,000 videos x 1 GB = 20 TB; 2 copies = 40 TB; 5x growth = 200 TB -> 4 x 16 TB HDDs now
HDD with 4 MB chunks: 121 MB/s effective; no cache needs 65 HDDs
With 80% cache hits: HDDs needed = 13; SSD traffic 5000 MB/s -> 1.7 NVMe drives (3 GB/s each)
With 90% cache hits: HDDs needed = 7; SSD traffic 5625 MB/s -> 1.9 NVMe drives (3 GB/s each)
Reads to start playing at minute m of a 1 GB video: extents = 1 extent-tree lookup (in inode) + 1 data read = 2; FAT/linked: up to 131072 look-ups/reads (mid-video: 1 GB/4 KB/2)
